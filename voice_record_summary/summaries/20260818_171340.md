## 会议总结

### 会议主题
本次会议围绕某产品（碟刹锁/immobilizer）在PVT阶段出现的点胶溢胶缺陷展开质量评审与根因分析，讨论临时纠正措施、长期改进方向、测试流程优化，以及相关尺寸配合问题，并明确后续报告更新与待办事项。

### 关键讨论

**1. 溢胶缺陷现象与根因**
- 点胶工艺用于保护电路板，正常情况下胶水不会流到齿轮区域；但不良品中胶水溢出到齿轮和滑槽部位，导致齿轮被抬高、功能失效。
- 移除螺丝后缺陷消失，确认问题根源来自点胶工艺过程控制不佳，而非设计本身直接导致。
- 客户质疑：加胶工艺并非新工艺，EVT、DVT阶段未发现此问题，为何PVT阶段突然出现。现场了解的情况是，前段时间部分部件生产繁忙，装配碟刹锁的人员有所变动（现有人员从其他工序调入，对当前产品不熟悉），可能是原因之一。

**2. 设计层面的溢胶风险**
- 现有设计有围挡，但因空间限制，围挡高度有限，无法完全防止胶水溢出。
- 六根线的出线口区域设计上允许少量溢胶，问题不大；两根开关线的出线口是最高风险点，靠线径与缺口卡死，实际效果不理想，且该处空间非常紧张，难以再加挡圈或挡块。
- 客户指出目前方案中未看到从设计上防止胶水流出的措施，要求从设计角度提出防溢方案。

**3. 临时纠正措施**
- 更新装配作业指导书（SOP），明确清洁流程的质量标准和清洁区域，使用照片标注清洁点位。
- 增加额外工位清除溢出的胶水，增加额外检查工位进行检测。
- 先用快干胶在出线口处封堵，再灌胶，防止胶水溢出；但该方案依赖操作员执行，存在人为疏忽风险。
- 增加额外压合夹具模拟实际装配后的压合状态，在最终装配测试中增加压合测试。

**4. 测试流程改进**
- 当前功能测试仅在自由状态下测试1个循环（锁/解锁），计划改进为对盖板施加压力（模拟装配到dropout的状态），每个零件至少测试5个循环，以提高缺陷检出率。
- 客户质疑为何需要5个循环而非1个循环；发言者解释标准测试是1个循环，但针对此新产品为更严格检查，前几批产品采用5循环测试以避免潜在问题，并非永久措施。
- 客户认为5循环测试只是临时捕获措施，建议还需找出根本原因并采取正确纠正措施。

**5. 人员培训与定岗问题**
- 人员变动并非招聘新员工，而是现有人员从其他产业或工序转入，对当前产品不熟悉、缺乏熟练度，涉及装后轮模块、装后端封装盒、电池锁等工序。
- 讨论是否定岗，但认为定岗也很难，只能作为一种说法/方案；担心以“人员调整”为由回复客户，客户可能认为以后都必须定岗。
- 确认会再安排一次培训，主要针对SOP相关风险点和改进后的作业指导书进行重新培训；培训必须有记录，要求每个人培训后签字确认。
- 发言者承认在新产品试产前未对员工进行足够培训以理解关键点，这是问题所在。

**6. 胶水固化工艺**
- 胶水固化条件为60度温度；产品灌胶后放置10分钟，再通过60度烘道烘干，烘道内至少经过半小时，但具体时间未精确记录。
- 烘道速度固定不可动，烘太久可能导致塑料件变形；建议在灌胶烘干的步骤中明确烘烤时间和温度（60度），使流程更清晰。
- 烘干后胶水不会再流动，除胶工序在烘干之后进行；从除胶到最终检测之间可能还有一段时间间隔，产品需分批组装，灌胶与不灌胶产品分开处理，装配总时间约需两天。

**7. 清洁与检查工位**
- 清洁工位要求操作员检查胶水并清洁胶水表面或内部，处理溢胶问题；列出四个关键控制点：四个感应点、针脚位置、PCB齿轮位置，这些区域若有溢胶需重点清除。
- 操作员进行视频检查胶水情况，操作员判断合格即合格，暂无他人复核。
- 工位顺序调整：将“检查溢胶/清洁是否到位”从第五项改为第一项，放在测试之前；操作员需确认前道工序质量，发现问题立即反馈上一工位；生产班长需检查上一工位操作员动作是否符合培训标准。

**8. 尺寸配合问题（immobilizer与dropout）**
- immobilizer的整体高度偏高，销钉位置在装配后不在dropout的中心；已测量过一件较小尺寸的样品，尺寸在8.23到8.43毫米之间，而immobilizer最小尺寸为8.5毫米，dropout约为8.23毫米，低于最小值。
- 如果尺寸过低，销钉可能完全被胶覆盖，影响未来功能；已发送视频和图片给对方，要求确保immobilizer激光孔和dropout孔位置匹配良好。
- immobilizer的模具已完成，很难重新制作新销钉，询问是否可能改变dropout的销钉位置；当前阶段很难有机会更改设计，建议增加特征来控制孔位，确保配合良好。
- 建议将dropout的检查结果加入AD报告中，分为功能和尺寸两个独立报告。

**9. 自动化点胶计划**
- 长期计划是在产量达到一定数量后引入自动化点胶机；目前产量不大，仍使用手动点胶，但会增加清除和检查工位。
- 目前缺少通过机器控制胶水，需要理解为什么胶会流出。

### 决策事项
- 更新SOP，明确清洁流程的质量标准和清洁区域，使用照片标注清洁点位。
- 增加额外清洁工位、检查工位和压合测试夹具。
- 将测试循环从1次增加到5次（前几批产品采用，非永久措施）。
- 将“检查溢胶/清洁是否到位”工位从第五项调整为第一项，放在测试之前。
- 再安排一次培训，针对SOP相关风险点和改进后的作业指导书，培训须有签字记录。
- 将溢胶问题纳入FACA报告，并提供根本原因；将dropout检查结果加入AD报告，分为功能和尺寸两个独立报告。
- 针对尺寸问题单独建立另一个AD报告，因为现有纠正措施对尺寸问题不正确。

### 待办事项
- 开启专项：研究并验证防止胶水流出的方案（从设计角度防溢），并验证方案有效性。
- 将“为何PVT阶段才出现溢胶问题”纳入问题清单，并在FACA报告中提供根因。
- 改进测试流程：加压状态下测试5个循环。
- 在灌胶烘干的步骤中明确烘烤时间和温度（60度），使流程更清晰。
- 跟进两份报告：法律报告和另一份报告（原文未明确具体名称）。
- 更新报告内容：增加清洁流程的纠正措施，以及循环测试。
- 要求对方发送基于doctor检查的图片和报告，以便一起审核dropout尺寸问题。
- 与机器人制造商讨论dropout设计更改的验证问题。

## English Summary

### Meeting Topic
This meeting focused on quality review and root cause analysis of a glue overflow defect found in a product (disc brake lock/immobilizer) during the PVT stage. Discussions covered temporary corrective actions, long-term improvement directions, test process optimization, related dimensional fit issues, and follow-up report updates and action items.

### Key Discussions

**1. Glue Overflow Defect and Root Cause**
- The dispensing process is intended to protect the circuit board; under normal conditions, glue should not reach the gear area. In defective units, glue overflowed onto the gear and slide slot areas, causing the gear to be lifted and function to fail.
- Removing the screw eliminated the defect, confirming that the root cause lies in poor process control of the dispensing operation rather than a direct design flaw.
- The customer questioned why this issue did not appear during EVT and DVT stages but suddenly emerged during PVT. On-site understanding indicated that personnel changes occurred recently due to production workload — existing staff were transferred from other processes and were unfamiliar with this product, which may be a contributing factor.

**2. Design-Level Overflow Risks**
- The current design includes a barrier wall, but due to space constraints, the wall height is limited and cannot fully prevent glue overflow.
- The six-wire outlet area allows minor overflow by design and is not a major concern; the two-wire (switch wire) outlet is the highest risk point, relying on wire diameter and notch fit, which has proven ineffective. The space is extremely tight, making it difficult to add additional barriers or blocks.
- The customer noted that no design-level measures to prevent glue outflow were seen in the current plan and requested design-based solutions.

**3. Temporary Corrective Actions**
- Updated the assembly SOP to clarify quality standards and cleaning areas for the cleaning process, using photos to mark cleaning points.
- Added an extra station to remove overflowed glue and an additional inspection station for detection.
- Applied quick-dry adhesive at the wire outlet before potting to prevent glue overflow; however, this solution depends on operator execution and carries human error risk.
- Added pressing fixtures to simulate the assembled state and incorporated pressing tests into final assembly testing.

**4. Test Process Improvement**
- Current functional testing only performs 1 cycle (lock/unlock) in a free state. The plan is to apply pressure to the cover (simulating dropout assembly) and test at least 5 cycles per part to improve defect detection.
- The customer questioned why 5 cycles are needed instead of 1. The speaker explained that the standard test is 1 cycle, but for this new product, stricter inspection with 5 cycles is applied for the first few batches as a temporary measure, not a permanent one.
- The customer noted that 5-cycle testing is only a temporary capture measure and recommended identifying the root cause and implementing proper corrective actions.

**5. Personnel Training and Job Assignment**
- Personnel changes involved existing employees transferred from other product lines or processes, not new hires. They were unfamiliar with the current product and lacked proficiency, affecting processes such as rear wheel module assembly, rear-end enclosure assembly, and battery lock.
- Discussion on whether to implement fixed job assignments concluded that fixed assignment is difficult and can only serve as a nominal approach. There was concern that citing "personnel changes" to the customer might lead to an expectation of permanent fixed assignments.
- Confirmed that another training session will be arranged, focusing on SOP-related risk points and the improved work instructions. Training must be documented with each person signing after completion.
- The speaker acknowledged that insufficient training was provided to employees before new product trial production to understand key points, which was a problem.

**6. Glue Curing Process**
- Glue curing condition is 60°C; after potting, products rest for 10 minutes, then pass through a 60°C curing oven for at least 30 minutes, though exact time was not precisely recorded.
- Oven speed is fixed and cannot be changed; excessive baking may cause plastic deformation. It was suggested to clearly specify baking time and temperature (60°C) in the potting/baking step for clarity.
- After baking, glue no longer flows; glue removal occurs after baking. There may be a time gap between glue removal and final inspection. Products must be processed in batches, with potted and non-potted products handled separately; total assembly time is approximately two days.

**7. Cleaning and Inspection Stations**
- The cleaning station requires operators to inspect glue and clean glue surfaces or interiors, addressing overflow issues. Four key control points were listed: four sensor points, pin positions, and PCB gear position — these areas require priority cleaning if overflow occurs.
- Operators perform video inspection of glue conditions; operator judgment is accepted as final with no secondary review currently.
- Station order was adjusted: "inspect overflow/cleaning adequacy" was moved from position 5 to position 1, before testing. Operators must confirm previous process quality and immediately report issues to the previous station; production line leaders must verify that previous station operators' actions meet training standards.

**8. Dimensional Fit Issues (Immobilizer vs. Dropout)**
- The immobilizer's overall height is too high, and the pin position is not centered in the dropout after assembly. A smaller sample was measured, with dimensions between 8.23 and 8.43 mm, while the immobilizer minimum dimension is 8.5 mm and the dropout measures approximately 8.23 mm, below the minimum.
- If dimensions are too low, the pin may be completely covered by glue, affecting future functionality. Videos and images were sent to the other party requesting that immobilizer laser holes and dropout holes be well aligned.
- The immobilizer mold is complete, making it difficult to remake new pins; the possibility of changing the dropout pin position was raised. At the current stage, design changes are difficult; it was suggested to add features to control hole positions to ensure proper fit and function.
- It was recommended to include dropout inspection results in the AD report, separated into functional and dimensional reports.

**9. Automated Dispensing Plan**
- The long-term plan is to introduce automated dispensing machines once production volume reaches a certain level. Currently, volume is low and manual dispensing is still used, but cleaning and inspection stations will be added.
- Machine-controlled glue application is currently lacking; there is a need to understand why glue flows out.

### Decisions
- Update the SOP to clarify quality standards and cleaning areas for the cleaning process, using photos to mark cleaning points.
- Add extra cleaning stations, inspection stations, and pressing test fixtures.
- Increase test cycles from 1 to 5 (applied to first few batches, not a permanent measure).
- Move the "inspect overflow/cleaning adequacy" station from position 5 to position 1, before testing.
- Arrange another training session focused on SOP-related risk points and improved work instructions, with signed training records.
- Include the glue overflow issue in the FACA report with root cause; include dropout inspection results in the AD report, separated into functional and dimensional reports.
- Create a separate AD report for dimensional issues, as existing corrective actions are not appropriate for the dimensional problem.

### Action Items
- Open a special initiative: research and validate solutions to prevent glue outflow (design-level prevention), and verify effectiveness.
- Add "why the overflow issue only appeared during PVT" to the issue list and provide root cause in the FACA report.
- Improve test process: perform 5 cycles under pressure.
- Clearly specify baking time and temperature (60°C) in the potting/baking step for process clarity.
- Follow up on two reports: the legal report and another report (specific name not stated in original text).
- Update report content: add corrective actions for the cleaning process and cycle testing.
- Request the other party to send images and reports based on doctor inspection for joint review of dropout dimensional issues.
- Discuss dropout design change verification with the robot manufacturer.
- Zny and La La are responsible for recording and updating the above items.
